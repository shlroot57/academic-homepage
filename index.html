About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Hangzhou Institute for Advanced Study (HIAS), University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), supervised by Prof. Taihao Li, Prof. Yuhua Zheng and Dr. Leyuan Qu. I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from HIAS, UCAS, in 2026, under the same supervision.

My research mainly focuses on interactive digital human generation, especially talking head synthesis that supports natural, expressive, and identity-preserving human–agent interaction. If you are interested in collaborating, feel free to reach out via email.

I greatly enjoy collaborating with Yu Liu and Haoxun Li.
